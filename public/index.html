TSW v2 1.2A bigger look

October 2026

  • Bigger TSW v2 title and a boxed search window.
  • Brighter, more colorful stars, plus tiny rockets that fly across the sky.
  • Larger icons and a reorganized settings panel, with a locked mod menu.
  • A list of features coming soon.

TSW v2 1.1Settings and shortcuts

October 2026

  • Settings now work: choose a search engine, turn stars and shooting stars on or off, and change the tab title and icon.
  • Add your own shortcuts with the + button. They are saved in your browser.
  • A waking-up screen shows while the server starts back up.

TSW v2 1.0Site launch

October 2026

  • TSW v2 goes live with a real web proxy built on the Scramjet engine.
  • Search bar and address bar, with back, forward, reload and home.
  • Shortcuts for TikTok, Instagram and GeForce NOW.
  • Server status in the bottom left corner.
